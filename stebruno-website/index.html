AI & MACHINE LEARNING RESEARCHER

Stefano Bruno.

Postdoctoral Research Associate, UNIST · Visiting Postdoctoral Researcher, University of Edinburgh

I work at the intersection of machine learning, optimization, and probability, developing theoretically grounded stochastic optimization and sampling algorithms for high-dimensional and nonconvex problems.

My research focuses on first- and zeroth-order optimization, Langevin dynamics, and diffusion-based generative modeling. My background in stochastic analysis informs my approach to both the theory and practice of modern AI.

01

Research

I study optimization and generative learning through the lens of stochastic processes, with an emphasis on mathematical guarantees and algorithms relevant to real-world machine learning.

Stochastic optimization

First- and zeroth-order methods, nonconvex optimization, flatness-aware learning, and stochastic-gradient algorithms.

Sampling & Langevin dynamics

Convergence analysis, stochastic differential equations, and efficient sampling in high dimensions.

Generative modeling

Score-based and diffusion-based generative models, error bounds, and Wasserstein convergence.

Applications & foundations

Machine learning theory, computer vision, and optimization problems motivated by industry and quantitative finance.
